# AtaPotify

**AtaPotify** is an Android app built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for splitting a shared **Spotify Family** subscription in **LKR** and tracking monthly listening stats with friends.

## Features
- **Monthly Payment Board**: Track each member's monthly LKR share (`Paid`, `Verifying`, or `Pending`), earn payment streak badges (`🔥`), view/share digital verified receipts, upload transfer slips, and receive cycle reminder notifications.
- **Spotify Leaderboard & Family Mix**: Connect Spotify to sync monthly listening minutes, top artists, and top tracks, and tap any artist or track in the **Family Mix** to open it in Spotify.
- **Approval Mode & Admin Tools**: Sign in with Google to request access, approve members from the Admin tab, pin live board announcements, browse billing history, and send WhatsApp reminders.
